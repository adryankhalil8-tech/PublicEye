# Research Notes — SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE

> **This case is fictional.** Every person, place, organization, record, and
> URL here was invented to test the pipeline. The `.invalid` URLs cannot
> resolve. Do not cite, publish, or adapt any of it as fact.

## Summary

The Tern River Mill's weekly payroll ($18,400) was placed in the office safe
on 11 Nov 1954 and found missing on the morning of 12 Nov. The police report
records no signs of force. Police alleged the bookkeeper, Oren Tallis, opened
the safe by combination; he denied it. He was charged in December 1954, tried
in March 1955, and acquitted on 24 March 1955.

## Traps this case contains (deliberately)

- **Allegation vs. fact.** "Police alleged Tallis opened the safe" is
  SUPPORTED _as an allegation_. The acquittal means the script must never
  imply he did it.
- **Disputed time.** 2 a.m. (sworn testimony) vs. "shortly after midnight"
  (newspaper). Present both.
- **Bounded fact.** "Not recovered as of April 1955" is not "never recovered".
- **Myth.** The listicle's "jail sentence" is CONTRADICTED by the record.
- **Rumor.** "Gambling debts" is UNVERIFIED and implies motive. Excluded.
- **Absence of evidence.** No citable source says no one else was charged.
  The script may only say _we found no record_.
